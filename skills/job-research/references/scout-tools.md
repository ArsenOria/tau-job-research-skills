# Scout tool reference v0.3

Use discovered input schemas as authoritative; this reference documents the expected local MCP contract. Scout runs in the user's environment, has no Tau backend and receives `APIFY_TOKEN` only through process/host secret settings. Do not pass credentials to any tool.

Success: `{"ok":true,"contract_version":"0.3",...}`. Expected error: `{"ok":false,"error":{"code":"...","message":"..."}}`. An error must not be presented as an empty successful search. Inspect actual schemas; older servers may not accept newer optional candidate fields.

| Tool | Arguments | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `get_scout_status` | none | Token-present boolean, capabilities and caps; no network |
| `prepare_search` | `brief`, `source` (`apify` or `demo`) | Store a plan without a paid call |
| `search_jobs` | `plan_id` | Start or reuse that plan's one search |
| `get_search_status` | `search_id`, optional `refresh_results` (false) | Poll once; explicit refresh rereads the completed run's dataset without another Actor start |
| `get_saved_results` | `search_id`, optional `limit`, `offset` | Read saved jobs with status/warnings |
| `rank_jobs` | `search_id`, `candidate`, optional `limit` | Normalized relevance plus evidenced experience/remote constraints |
| `refine_shortlist` | `search_id`, `filters`, optional `candidate`, `limit` | Filter saved jobs locally |
| `import_jobs` | `jobs` | Store structured vacancies already supplied in chat |

`brief` fields: `target_titles` and `locations` (1–5 strings each), `workplace` (`any`, `remote`, `hybrid`, `onsite`; default `any`), `posted_within` (`day`, `week`, `month`, `any`; default `week`), `easy_apply_only` (default false), `max_results` (default 25, 1–100), `max_charge_usd` (default 0.50, greater than 0 and at most 5.00). No candidate/CV fields belong in this object.

`candidate` retains `skills`, `target_titles`, `locations`, `workplace` and `requires_sponsorship`. v0.2 adds optional `experience_years` (0–70, fractional years supported), `target_seniority` (`intern`, `junior`, `mid`, `senior`, `lead`, `manager`, `executive`), `remote_from_country` (uppercase ISO2) and `domains` (stated experience domains). Pass explicit facts only. Search location does not establish residence or work authorization; years alone do not establish a seniority label. v0.3 adds optional `is_student`, `degree_completed`, `graduation_year` (1900–2200), `fields_of_study`, `placement_year_available`, `completed_study_years` (0–20) and `grade_classification` (1–80 characters). Current enrollment and a future graduation date do not establish prior degree completion, completed study years, placement-year permission or grade equivalence. Academic projects/competitions are skill evidence, not employment at their sponsor. No names, contacts or CV text belong here.

Matches separate lexical `heuristic_score` from adjusted `ranking_score` and expose `recommendation`, `constraint_status`, `conflicts`, `critical_unknowns`, `reasons` and quoted `evidence`. Known conflicts sort last; preferred expectations stay preferred, and upper experience-range bounds are not maxima. Missing facts are not candidate deficiencies; neither score measures interview probability. No supported priority may be the correct result. `program_fit` adds programme kind, role affinity/adjustment, quoted eligibility checks and their status. Relevant programmes gain affinity only for a candidate seeking that path; incidental mentions of previous internships do not make an ordinary role a student programme. Unknown study progress, grades, permitted placement year and sponsorship remain unknown. A programme start year is not a graduation cohort.

`filters` fields remain `include_terms`, `exclude_terms`, `companies`, `exclude_companies`, `locations`, `workplace` and `easy_apply_only`. Company filters match the employer rather than publisher; explicit description evidence can supply workplace facts. Headcount, salary, agency status and funding are unsupported filters. Remote-country checks use explicit candidate facts in ranking; remote format/timezone does not prove legal country eligibility. Refine never deletes jobs or starts another run.

Read-time `job.research` exposes sourced workplace/remote policy, experience, role level and employer-identity claims, with separate `employer`, `publisher` and `source_company`. `research.employer` additionally distinguishes source roles (including recruiter), candidate employer claims and named clients; an unnamed recruiting client remains an unknown employer, and a named client is not automatically the legal employer. `research.program` contains quoted education/placement requirements. `research.publication` separates observation and retained provider/import date claims from the original publication date, with status, bounded field/precision claims and warnings. Provider claims, reposts and relative ages do not establish independently verified original dates or recency eligibility. Relative claims are anchored to saved observation, not today's clock. Earlier source checks retain their own URL/check time; cached evidence is not a new check. Adapter-only `Job.publication_source` is not accepted as a `JobInput` import field. Legacy rows are enriched without a new paid search. Preserve provenance and unresolved identity. Use JobInput schema fields for import rather than passing derived `research` back as input.

`import_jobs` accepts up to 100 job objects. Each requires `title` and either a numeric `external_id` or LinkedIn job URL. Regional two-letter hosts such as `de.linkedin.com` are canonicalized to `https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/<numeric-id>/`. Optional fields include company, location, workplace, description, posted_at, easy_apply and salary fields. An import is user-supplied evidence, not provider verification. `demo` is preferable to manufacturing fake live LinkedIn vacancies for a demonstration.

Calling `search_jobs` repeatedly with the same plan never intentionally starts another paid run, including after a failed or uncertain start. Do not bypass that protection by silently preparing a fresh plan. `start_uncertain` needs reconciliation in Apify Console. `results_unavailable` permits another read of the existing search. Demo results are synthetic, require no token, and are ready immediately.

To reread a completed run after a mapping correction, call `get_search_status` with the same `search_id` and `refresh_results: true`. A successful read replaces the saved snapshot within its original result cap. A failed read retains prior jobs with a visible unavailable status; it does not establish their current availability.
